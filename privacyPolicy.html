Privacy Policy

About this project

Join is a kanban-based task management tool built as a student project at the Developer Akademie GmbH. It is an educational exercise and not intended for extensive personal or business use. This policy explains which data the app processes and why.

Which data we process

Account

When you sign up we store your name, e-mail address and password so you can log in again. If you use the guest login, no account is created.

Tasks and contacts

Tasks (title, description, due date, priority, category, subtasks, assigned contacts) and contacts (name, e-mail address, phone number) that you create are stored so they are available on all your devices and to the users you share them with.

Guest mode

In guest mode tasks and contacts are kept only in the local storage of your browser and are removed when you log out. Nothing is sent to our servers.

Hosting and storage (Firebase)

Join is hosted with Google Firebase and stores your data in the Firebase Realtime Database. Firebase is a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. When you use the app, technical data such as your IP address and browser information is processed by Google in order to deliver the pages. Details can be found in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Cookies and local storage

Join does not use cookies or tracking tools. Your login state and guest data are kept in the local storage of your browser and can be removed at any time by logging out or clearing your browser data.

Your rights

You can view and edit your contacts and tasks in the app at any time. On request we will inform you about the data stored for your account, correct it or delete your account together with all associated data. Please contact us at rloske90@gmail.com.
