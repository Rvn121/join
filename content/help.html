Welcome to the help page for Join, your guide to using our kanban project management tool.

What is Join?

Join is a kanban-based project management tool for organizing tasks, contacts and project progress.

How to use it

  1. Exploring the Board – View and organize project tasks.
  2. Creating Contacts – Add people for future task assignments.
  3. Adding Cards – Create tasks and define their details.
  4. Moving Cards – Move tasks through the kanban workflow.
